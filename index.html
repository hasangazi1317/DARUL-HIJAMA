Why people choose cupping

Many people turn to cupping for comfort and relaxation. It is a complementary therapy and results vary from person to person.

  • Relief from tight, stiff muscles
  • Neck, shoulder and back comfort
  • A feeling of deep relaxation
  • Support for healthy circulation
  • Ease from tension headaches

Questions, answered

Does cupping hurt?

Most people feel a pulling or tight sensation that settles quickly. We adjust the suction to your comfort.

How long does a session take?

Usually 30 to 60 minutes including consultation, depending on the type of cupping.

Will it leave marks?

Round marks are common and normally fade within a few days to a couple of weeks.

Who should avoid cupping?

Please tell us if you are pregnant, take blood thinners, have a bleeding disorder, a skin infection or a serious illness. We may ask you to check with your doctor first.
